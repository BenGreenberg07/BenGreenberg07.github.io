Cantilever Truss: Design, Build, and Load Test

Swarthmore College, ENGR 006 · April 2026

Designed, machined, and load-tested a 6.45 lb aluminum cantilever truss that carried a 1,000 lb point load. Traced a 3x deflection error (0.12 in predicted, 0.39 in measured) to joint misalignment, not member sizing.

Nine-Key Macropad, Custom PCB

Independent project, Hack Club Blueprint · May 2026

code & PCB

Designed the schematic and 2-layer PCB in KiCad for a 9-key macropad built around a Seeed XIAO RP2040, with Cherry MX switches and addressable RGB. Hand-soldered it, 3D-printed the enclosure, and flashed custom KMK firmware, so it works as a plug-and-play USB keyboard with fully programmable keys.
